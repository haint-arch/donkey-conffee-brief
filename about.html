Coffee Story Field Notes

Story

A roastery in the city.

Raw, dusty, and honest. Donkey doesn't chase glamour — we just make decent coffee.

Manifesto

Good coffee needn't be complicated. Just honest.

The name Donkey is a nod to the stamina and free spirit of people who love to roam. We roast in small batches, hand-pick every single-origin lot, and tell the story of the farmer behind each cup.

Space

Space

Raw, unadorned.

Exposed metal ducting, rough timber walls, warm workshop lights. We keep the raw 'workshop' feel so you can watch each batch roasted right in front of you — transparent from green bean to cup.

People

People

Barista and guest face the same way.

At Donkey, the counter isn't a wall between us. The barista weighs beans while telling you about the latest Gesha lot; regulars chime in on extraction. Everyone faces the same thing: flavor.
