Brewing

Extract the single origin right

01

Pour-over / V60

15g coffee : 250ml water at 92–94°C, poured in 3 stages over 2:30 — brings out florals & fruit.

02

Espresso

18g in : 40g out in 28–32s. Deep sweet finish, round body.

03

Aeropress

14g : 200ml, steep 1:30 then press slowly — light and travel-friendly.
